### Story components as electrical patterns for exploring the analogy 

Exposition - is essentially describing the existing state using StatePrimitives. Main character is a sloth etc.
Voltage - is potential vs actual, he has big dreams 
Current - is how fixed he is in his ways, how hard that inertia will be to overcome
Incite to Action - is a change knob, perhaps a switch closing.
Building Tension - is like loading a capacitor, building potential, but not yet changing the state.
Obstacles - are resistors, slowing change, making it harder to reach the goal.
Conflict - is a non-linear element, like a transistor that changes the way the system behaves in a non-linear way. An if-else.
Tempo - is frequency, the pacing of the story, how often things happen, how fast the character reacts.
Subplot - is a parallel circuit, another story arc that may or may not interact with the main one.
Climax - is a peak before the tipping, perhaps AC peak, or max power draw before transistor flips.
Ground - is the resolution, the return to a stable state, resolving an obstacle or the end of the story arc.
Lesson - the algorithm learned from probing the story space, the updated model of the world.
Theme - the pattern that is being explored that can apply to other spaces. 
Genre - the type of space/field being probed. Electricity vs Mechanics vs Fluid motion etc
